The Bishop has given the city to the Vigil. Curfew from the evening bell. Every door barred, every lamp lit, and in every street the hunters walk in fours and fives, shoulder to shoulder, a man at the back who never stops looking behind him.
They have drawn a ring around the cathedral. Three squads hold it: one on Mercy Bridge, one in the market under the searchlight, one in the close before the west doors.
A squad is one animal. Take a man and the rest close up, back to back, and hunt you together. But they share one nerve between them. Shake it, man by man, lamp by lamp, until it breaks, and they will run like anyone else.
Break all three, and the way to the west doors is open. The Abbess is waiting underneath.
Be inside before dawn.

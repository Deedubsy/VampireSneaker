The Riverside Gasworks. Every gas lamp in the city drinks from its holder; every white lamp the Council hangs to burn you is made in its manufactory.
Three consignments of sunstone lamps ship at dawn: one crated in the manufactory, one on a dray in the loading yard, one on the wharf waiting for the barge. Burn them.
Then the great gas holder. Set a fuse in the valve house at its foot. When it goes, the city goes dark, and stays dark.
The works are guarded, and the Council has put up searchlights: arc lamps on towers, worked by men who see everything the light touches. Kill the man and the lamp stops where it is. The engine house on the wharf powers all three.
The night shift is still working. A gas holder takes a great deal with it when it goes. Get them out, or don't.
Tobias will hold the boat at the west end of the wharf.

#! M08 — Hollin's Hunt. Act II "Hunted": Marrowmere, the drowned fen village Ilse was born in. Sister Maud Hollin,
#! the Vigil's tracker, has come with her hounds because "they always go home". She hunts: every so often she takes a
#! fix on Ilse's scent and walks the pack to where Ilse was. Rain squalls come and go: in rain her nose is half as
#! good, trails wash away, footfalls are quiet; in the lulls she closes fast. The mission is the hunt turned round:
#! kill Hollin, or feed on her (her ward charm tears) and take her for a thrall, then leave by the drove road.
#! Layout (west → east):
#!   The causeway (start SW) and Old Tamsin's hut. The reed beds (concealment; the peat store) behind the peat drain
#!     (running water), crossed only by the plank bridge on the reed path, which can be sawn through under Hollin.
#!   The flood dyke along the north (a Vigil hunter and hound walk it).
#!   The drowned village: shallow water to the knee, cottages whose roofs are a leap apart (ivy on the corners),
#!     St Aud's church and its tower (the fifth shrine at the top), the Marrow house (a candle in the window:

#!     vampire's crossing), the sluice bridge (the sluice can be opened under whoever crosses next).
#!   Hollin's camp on the hummock: the tithe barn (her journal), tents, the kennels, the drove road north (the exit).

Marrowmere. The village you were born in, two days' walk across the fens from Ostmere.
The dyke broke the winter you were nine. The water came up the lanes in a night and never went down again. Everyone left. You left. You did not think you would come back.
Sister Maud Hollin of the Pale Vigil thought you would. She is camped in the tithe barn across the Marrow Cut with her hounds, and she has lit a candle in your mother's window.
She will hunt you tonight. Let her. A hunter who comes to you can be met. Kill her, or open her and take her, and walk out by the drove road.
The squalls are coming in off the mere. Rain drowns a scent.

The Coldwater Institute. You died here. You came back up through its drains with nothing, and ran.
You are not running now.
Saule has gone below with his serum and his Abbess. His staff are clearing the upper wards. At first light, the subjects who failed will be taken down to the coal yard and burned under the sunstone lamps, as you were meant to be.
There are three of them left: fledglings, as you were, starved and in chains. And there is Clement, the clerk who wrote "deceased" on your record and let you be forgotten. Saule found out. He is in the quiet room.
Break their chains. Lead them out to Tobias, who is waiting with a Guild cart at the west end of Coldwater Lane. They cannot climb. The fledglings will not walk into sunstone light, and it will burn them if they do.

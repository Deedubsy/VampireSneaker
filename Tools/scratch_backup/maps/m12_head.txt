St Calder's island. The monks kept lepers here, then heretics. Now the Pale Vigil keeps it, and in the archive over the keep's great hall Captain Vane keeps everything he knows about you.
Every lamp you put out. Every throat. Every rooftop. He has read it all, and tonight, in his own house, he has prepared for all of it.
Take the Dossier. Then decide what Vane is worth: kill him under his own roof, or leave him alive and burn every page he has.
Three ways across the water. The old weir to the west ledge, below the prison and its outfall. The Vigil boatman's skiff to the water-stair. Or the causeway, if Brother Anselm can be persuaded to wind up the portcullis: he is a lay brother, not a hunter, and wears no charm.
The archive is lit by sunstone. The grid is fed from a generator in the keep.
The Faithful he has taken are in his cells. Their boat waits below the prison postern.

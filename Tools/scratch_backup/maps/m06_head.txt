Highgate Rise, above the river. Snow on the box hedges.
Penrose talked, in the end. The Lantern-Master's orders reach her through Lord Ashcombe, and tonight Lord Ashcombe holds his winter masque. Half the Council of Lanterns will be under his roof, behind masks, in the one room in Ostmere where no one asks who anyone is.
Neither will they ask who you are, with a mask and an invitation. Listen. Learn their faces. Then find what Ashcombe keeps in his study.
The house is a home. You cannot cross its threshold uninvited, and the Vigil's captain is a guest.

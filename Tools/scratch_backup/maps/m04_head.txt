Ostmere. The eve of Saint Corvin's.
The fever ward of Saint Brigid's sent fourteen patients to the Coldwater Institute, and none of them were buried. You were the ninth. Somebody wrote those names down: Deacon Thorne keeps the register in his archive at Saint Corvin's.
The Cathedral Quarter guards itself with bells. Three towers, three ropes: a frightened priest or a watchman on any one of them can wake the whole quarter.
Cut the ropes. Then take the register.

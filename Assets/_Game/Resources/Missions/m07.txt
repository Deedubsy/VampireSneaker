#! M07 — The Toll Bridges. Act II "Traverse / Escape": cross the river district on the night the bridges closed and
#! reach the fen road. Running water cuts the map into four banks; every crossing is a checkpoint.
#! Teaches the Pale Vigil in force: hunters (look up, flares, silver), hounds (smell through darkness, follow blood
#! trails, walk at their handler's heel), checkpoints, and Lockdown reinforcements that now bring hunter and hound.
#! Layout (west → east):
#!   Tanners' Row (start SW among the tanning pits). The Mill Leat: three crossings — the Leat Bridge (Watch
#!     barricade, a sergeant and two men), the leat-house roofs (drying-shed downpipe on the Row, or the back-lane
#!     downpipe south; a Vigil hunter at the island end looks up), or the mill, whose wheel room straddles the leat
#!     (the miller and a watchman asleep; the fourth shrine is in the wheel pit).
#!   Chandlers' Island: the Wick (shut for curfew, not empty), the chapel of Saint Ide (holy light), the Watch house,
#!     a hunter with a hound at heel. The River Ost: two crossings — the toll bridge (chained; the lever in the
#!     tollhouse beside the keeper, loud; a thrall can pull it; or climb the toll arch in the lamplight) or the
#!     ferryman's skiff on the south quay (sealed until the ferryman is dazed, enthralled or dead).
#!   Ostbank Wharf: the Vigil holds the bridge foot (two hunters, a hound, braziers). The Vigil guard hut (the field
#!     manual; the warden and a hunter talk about Sister Hollin), the Faithful's pen (Rumour), the causeway.
#!   The Fen Cut: two crossings — the causeway (hunters at both ends, braziers) or the lock-gate catwalk in the dark
#!     north (a hound chained by the lock-keeper's door). The fens: reeds, a hunter and hound on the dyke path, the
#!     fen road east (the exit).

Ostmere, the river district. Three nights after the masque.
Vane has your name now, and he has closed the city on it. Every bridge is a checkpoint: the Watch holds the Leat, the toll bridge over the Ost is chained, and the Vigil keeps the Fen Cut causeway with its own hunters and their hounds.
Beyond the Cut is the fen road, and beyond the fens is Marrowmere, the village you were born in. Nobody has lived there since the water came. Nobody will look for you in a drowned village.
Running water will not let you pass. Bridges, boats and roofs that span the water will.

on interact manual: campaign_flag vigil_manual ; say Ilse "Their ledger. They're counting me." ; lore vigil_manual "The Vigil field manual" "Vane's people keep a ledger of every night: how Ilse fed, killed, was seen. A habit entered three nights running is answered with a countermeasure. Change the habit and the answer cools." ; hint "The Dossier in the night hub now shows exactly how the Vigil weighs your habits, and what they'll answer next."
on interact lv_pen if rumour: route f1 r_free ; route f2 r_free ; route f3 r_free ; bark f1 "Lady. Lady, bless you." ; rumour 1 ; timer t_freed 20
on timer t_freed: remove f1 ; remove f2 ; remove f3
on enter z_lock: say Ilse "The lock gates. A catwalk across the Cut, and a dog on a chain."
on enter z_fens: checkpoint "The Fen Cut" ; say Ilse "The fens. It smells like home. I hate that it smells like home."
on evidence trail: hint "A hound has your blood trail and will follow it to its end. Mist leaves no drops, and the wound closes if you rest in the dark."
on lockdown: say Ilse "Whistles on every bank." ; hint "Lockdown. The Vigil answers with hunters and hounds as well as the Watch."
on complete road: say Ilse "The fen road. Marrowmere is at the end of it." ; say Abbess "And so am I, daughter. Nearly."

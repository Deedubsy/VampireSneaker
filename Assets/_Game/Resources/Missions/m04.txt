#! M04 — The Bells of Saint Corvin. Act I "Sabotage": silence the Cathedral Quarter's three alarm bells, then steal
#! the fever-ward registry from the deacon's archive. End of Act I.
#! Teaches holy light (votives, altar lamps; priests' auras block abilities within 6 m), bells as alarms (an alerted
#! priest or unarmed acolyte runs to the nearest live bell within 20 m; a rung bell is a district lockdown), and
#! verticality: towers, pipes, aisle roofs and continuous wall tops.
#! Layout: the Weir along the south (start SW on the ferry steps, escape SE on the Weir steps). West — the Pilgrims'
#!   Lane lodging houses and Saint Brigid's almshouse yard with Bell A in its tower (votives at the door; the
#!   almshouse roof and yard wall run to the tower's wall top). Centre — the cathedral: nave (altar lamp, side-chapel
#!   votives), the Great Bell tower on its west front (Bell B; a Watch post outside; the west aisle roof drops onto
#!   its wall top), the deacon's archive at the north end, the walled anchoress's cell behind it. East — the Bishop's
#!   Close, and the Watch-house with Bell C in its tower and a walled yard (the stable roof reaches the yard wall).
#! The registry is sealed until all three bells are cut; then Deacon Thorne leaves the archive to inspect the
#!   Great Bell, and returns periodically.

Ostmere. The eve of Saint Corvin's.
The fever ward of Saint Brigid's sent fourteen patients to the Coldwater Institute, and none of them were buried. You were the ninth. Somebody wrote those names down: Deacon Thorne keeps the register in his archive at Saint Corvin's.
The Cathedral Quarter guards itself with bells. Three towers, three ropes: a frightened priest or a watchman on any one of them can wake the whole quarter.
Cut the ropes. Then take the register.

@script
on start: say Ilse "Saint Corvin's. Where they brought the fever-ward dead, and wrote down their names." ; say Ilse "Mine will be in there. Somewhere in the deacon's books." ; timer t_intro 5
on timer t_intro: hint "Three bells guard this quarter. An alarmed priest, acolyte or watchman runs to the nearest bell, and one ring locks the whole district down. Cut each rope {Interact} before you go for the register."
on enter z_lane: hint "Drainpipes lead onto the roofs. From the lane, a pipe reaches the cathedral's west aisle roof, and from there the wall tops run all around the nave."
on enter z_brigid: say Ilse "Votive candles at the tower door. Even from here they sting." ; hint "Holy light burns: votives, altar lamps and wayside shrines hurt Ilse like sunlight. Priests carry it inside them. No ability works within 6 m of a priest."
on enter z_towerA: say Ilse "The ringer is asleep on his rope. Good boy, Pim."
on interact bA: say Ilse "Saint Brigid's is mute."
on interact bB: say Ilse "The Great Bell. Two tons of bronze, and one knife."
on interact bC: say Ilse "The Watch can shout all it likes now."
on enter z_roofW: say Ilse "The aisle roof runs the length of the nave. They never look up in a church; they're too busy looking up at God."
on enter z_nave: say Ilse "Saint Corvin's. I was christened here." ; hint "Watch the altar lamp and the side-chapel votives. Pews and pillars cast deep shadow."
on enter z_archive: say Ilse "The deacon's archive. Every name in the quarter, and what became of it."
on enter z_wh: say Ilse "Sergeant Pell. He signed the notice about the body-snatcher."
on enter z_stable: hint "The stable roof leans against the Watch yard wall. Walk the wall top to the bell tower."
on enter z_cell: say Abbess "You found her cell. She sat here forty years, listening to the floor." ; say Ilse "Listening to you." ; say Abbess "Everyone listens to me, eventually."
on secret s_cell: vitae 10 ; lore anchoress "The anchoress of Saint Corvin's" "A cell bricked shut behind the cathedral's chapter house. Carved above the cot: MOTHER BELOW. In newer chalk: SHE IS UNDER THE CATHEDRAL."
on interact registry: say Ilse "Fourteen names. 'Released to the Institute. Not to be buried.'" ; say Ilse "Ilse Marrow. Nineteen. Seamstress." ; say Ilse "Not to be buried. No. I don't suppose I will be." ; reveal away ; checkpoint "The register" ; lore corvin_register "The Saint Corvin register" "Fourteen fever-ward patients of Saint Brigid's, released by Deacon Thorne to Dr. Saule of the Coldwater Institute. 'Not to be buried.' The Council of Lanterns paid forty guineas for the service. Ilse's is the ninth name." ; campaign_flag weirside_beast
on complete bells: say Ilse "Three ropes cut. Nobody in this quarter can call for help tonight." ; unseal registry ; route thorne r_thorne ; timer t_thorne 2
on timer t_thorne: bark thorne "The bells... someone has been at the bells. I must see the Great Bell for myself." ; hint "Deacon Thorne has left his archive to inspect the Great Bell. He won't stay away long."
on enter z_archive if !bells: hint "The register sits under the deacon's hand. Cut all three bell ropes first: he'll go to see what happened, and nobody will be able to raise the quarter."
on complete registry: hint "East along the quay to the Weir steps. The Watch yard gate opens onto them."
on lockdown: say Ilse "The bells. The whole quarter is awake." ; hint "Lockdown: every guard in the district is searching. Get out of sight and wait for it to pass."
on spotted any: hint "Seen! A frightened priest or acolyte will run for the nearest bell that still has a rope. Stop them, or cut it first."
on kill thorne: say Ilse "Deacon Thorne. Forty guineas for fourteen souls. I'd say we're square."
on feed thorne: say Ilse "Scalding. Even rotten, a priest's blood burns."
on interact n_brigid: say Ilse "'At the Deacon's invitation.' He sold us."
on interact n_order: say Ilse "The Weirside Beast. That's what they're calling me."
on interact n_rota: say Ilse "One rope and the whole quarter rings. Then I'll take all three."
on complete away: say Ilse "Let them ring for the Weirside Beast. I am done running from bells."

@script
on start: say Ilse "Ostmere docks. Salt, tar, and a thousand sailors' hearts." ; say Ilse "The Institute ships its cargo from here. The Harbourmaster's books will say where to." ; timer t_intro 6 ; timer t_dream 150
on timer t_intro: hint "Blood has qualities. Drunk sailors make you languid and quiet; fevered beggars sharpen your senses. Feeding on a new kind of blood reveals what it does."
on enter z_market: say Ilse "The night catch. Fishwives, sailors, beggars by the braziers. Everyone hungry."
on enter z_bridge: say Ilse "The Ost. I can feel it pulling at me, like a tide in my chest." ; hint "Running water cannot be crossed: not swimming, not leaping. Use a bridge, a boat, or a roof that spans it."
on enter z_wquay: hint "A waterman's skiff. Use it {Interact} to row across the harbour mouth to the east quay."
on enter z_alley: hint "Drainpipes lead onto the net-shed roofs. The sheds run all the way to the Customs Arch over the Ost."
on enter z_roofs: say Ilse "Candlelight, down among the sheds. Walled in on every side."
on enter z_arch: say Ilse "The Customs Arch. The river runs under me and cannot touch me."
on enter z_yard: say Ilse "Institute crates. Forty of them, waiting for a ship."
on enter z_plaza: say Ilse "The Harbourmaster's office. A lamp upstairs, at this hour."
on enter z_upstairs: say Ilse "Harbourmaster Quill. He keeps late hours, and he keeps them for the Institute."
on enter z_equay: hint "Carry a body [C] to the water's edge and drop it. The harbour keeps what it's given."
on feed any: flag dream
on timer t_dream: flag dream
on flag dream: say Abbess "Still chasing them through the dark, daughter? I told you. Call them, and they will come." ; say Ilse "I didn't ask for your help." ; say Abbess "You will. The river cannot be crossed, but men can be sent across it." ; grant dominion.beckon ; timer t_beckon 4
on timer t_beckon: hint "Beckon [1] reaches across the water: call a man off the far bank's post, or away from the bridge lamps, without ever setting foot in the light."
on interact manifest: say Ilse "'Paid in full by the Council of Lanterns.' And the Saint Corvin registry, sent to Saule." ; say Ilse "Fever-ward patients. Like me." ; reveal away ; checkpoint "The manifest" ; lore ardent_manifest "The Ardent's manifest" "The Institute ships forty crates of 'salted specimens' to Halvard on the brig Ardent, paid for by the Council of Lanterns. The Saint Corvin registry of fever-ward patients went with them, for Dr. Saule's own hand."
on complete manifest: hint "Back down the old pier, where you came in. The bridge, the skiff, or the roofs."
on secret s_shrine: campaign_flag found_shrine1 ; say Abbess "You found my little altar. They still remember me, the drowned ones." ; say Ilse "Who lit these candles?" ; say Abbess "Someone who listens. As you will." ; vitae 10 ; lore abbess_shrine "Mother Below" "Hidden among the net sheds of the Ostmere docks, a shrine of fish-bone crowns and candle stubs. Scratched into the mortar: MOTHER BELOW. Someone still tends it."
on interact v_bridge: say Ilse "The bridge lamps are dying." ; bark w2 "Bridge lamps are out! Someone fetch the lamplighter. Oi, you, stay sharp!"
every interact boat_w: ferry 50 35 ; say Ilse "Across the mouth of the Ost, low and quiet." 
every interact boat_e: ferry 27 35
on interact n_letter: say Ilse "'They knock at night.' Of course they do."
on interact n_crate: say Ilse "Twelve of us in each box. Some still breathing."
on interact n_harbour: say Ilse "'Not to be opened by Customs.' Quill is bought."
on feed quill: say Ilse "Ink and brandy and fear. The Harbourmaster tastes like paperwork."
on kill quill: say Ilse "The Council will need a new Harbourmaster."
on dispose any: say Ilse "The harbour keeps what it's given."
on spotted any: hint "Seen! Break their line of sight and get into the dark. A watchman who loses you will search, then give up."
on kill any: hint "Bodies get found. Hide them, or carry them [C] to the water."

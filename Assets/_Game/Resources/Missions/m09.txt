#! M09 — Coldwater, Above. Act III "Predator" opens where it all began: the Coldwater Institute, the upper wards
#! this time. Saule has gone down to the Undercroft with the Abbess; his staff are clearing the upper floor, and
#! at dawn the subjects who failed will be "cleansed" under the sunstone in the coal yard. Ilse comes back for the
#! people still in it: Clement, the clerk who amended her record, and three fledglings, the subjects who came after her.
#! Teaches escort (they follow on foot, can't climb, are recaptured if a guard reaches them) and sunstone lamps

#! Layout:
#!   Coldwater Lane (south, start east; Tobias's cart at the west end is the way out), the coal yard (west: the
#!     subjects' route, under a sunstone) and the main gate (sunstone, Vigil hunters), the physic garden, the
#!     generator house (east: the breaker for every sunstone lamp, and its engineer).
#!   The upper floor: the long gallery; Ward C (west: three cells on a sunstone-lit corridor, the orderlies' station);
#!     Saule's laboratory (centre, an observation gallery above); the east wing (library, Clement's quiet room,
#!     the dispensary, Saule's study with his notes).

on start: say Ilse "Coldwater." ; say Abbess "You came back to the womb, daughter." ; say Ilse "I came back for the others." ; timer t_intro 6
on timer t_intro: hint "The white lamps are sunstone. Their light burns you: it can't be snuffed by hand. Cut them all at the generator in the east of the grounds, or have a thrall smash one." ; timer t_intro2 14
on timer t_intro2: hint "Prisoners follow you on foot once freed: they can't climb, and a guard who reaches one drags them back to their chains. Click a freed prisoner to make them wait or follow."
on enter z_out: say Tobias "Ilse? I've the cart. Who are we taking?"
on enter z_yard: checkpoint "The coal yard" ; say Ilse "The coal yard. This is where they meant to burn me."
on enter z_garden: say Ilse "Saule's physic garden. Nightshade and garlic. He always did like a joke."
on enter z_gen: say Ilse "The generator. Every white lamp on the grounds hangs off this."
on interact gen: hint "The sunstone lamps are dark. The engineer will come to restore the current as soon as anyone notices: deal with him, or move fast."
on enter z_gallery: checkpoint "The upper floor"
on enter z_ward: say Ilse "Ward C. I can hear them breathing. Barely."
on enter z_lab: say Ilse "His laboratory. I was on that slab." ; say Abbess "And I was in the jar beside it."
on enter z_quiet: say Ilse "The quiet room."
on interact clem.free: say Clement "They said you were a monster." ; say Ilse "They were right. Walk behind me and do exactly what I say." ; hint "Clement follows you. Lead him out of the west door of the gallery, through the coal yard, to Tobias's cart."
on interact f1.free: say Ilse "Twelve. Up. Come on."
on interact f2.free: say Ilse "Thirteen. I know. I know. Not yet."
on interact f3.free: say Ilse "Fourteen. You're the last one. Stay close."
on interact f1.free: hint "A fledgling will not walk into sunstone light. Cut the lamps on its path first. Or press Drain over it to turn it loose: it hunts the staff until someone puts it down."
on interact clem.out: say Tobias "Up you get. Mind the lamp oil." ; say Clement "Thank you. Both of you. Thank you." ; campaign_flag clement_saved
on interact f1.out: say Tobias "God. It's... they're children, Ilse." ; say Ilse "They were."
on interact f2.out: say Tobias "In the back. Under the tarpaulin."
on interact f3.out: say Tobias "That's three. Ilse, what do I do with them?" ; say Ilse "Keep them out of the light. Feed them rats. I'll come."
on interact f1.loose: say Abbess "Yes. Let them eat." ; terror 1
on interact f2.loose: say Abbess "Go on, little one." ; terror 1
on interact f3.loose: say Abbess "That is what they are for." ; terror 1
on interact n_register: say Ilse "'Deceased. Record closed. C.' He saved me with a pen."
on interact n_clement: say Ilse "It was only ink. It was enough."
on interact study_key: say Ilse "Saule's study. His notes."
on interact notes: say Ilse "Every page." ; say Abbess "He has copies, daughter. He has me." ; lore saule_notes "Saule's notes" "Saule's working notes on the eternal-vitae serum, burned in his study at Coldwater. The serum is drawn from the Abbess's blood, which 'does not travel': he must bleed her where she lies, in a crypt under the cathedral." ; campaign_flag saule_notes_burned
on interact c_station: say Ilse "Number nine. That's what I am to them."
on interact c_lab: say Ilse "Under the cathedral." ; lore undercroft "The undercroft" "The Abbess's blood does not travel. Saule has taken his work down to her, into a crypt older than the city, under the cathedral." ; campaign_flag knows_undercroft
on secret s_shrine: vitae 10 ; say Abbess "I was the first. Before Saule. Before his father." ; lore abbess_first "The sixth shrine" "On the observation rail over Saule's laboratory: 'I was here first. I was the first subject. She has always been downstairs.'" ; campaign_flag found_shrine6

on complete subjects: say Ilse "That's the subjects." ; hint "Now get out yourself: the west end of the lane."
on fail clem: say Ilse "Clement... no."
on fail subjects: say Ilse "Too many. I was too slow."
on lockdown: say Ilse "Bells. The whole Institute's awake." ; hint "Lockdown. Vigil hunters will come through the main gate."
on complete out: say Ilse "Drive, Tobias." ; say Abbess "Downstairs, daughter. I am always downstairs." ; win

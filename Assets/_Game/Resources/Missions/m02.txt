Ostmere. The night after you died.
Tobias lodges at Mother Grice's on Lantern Street: the tall house at the east end, by the Watch post. He is a lamplighter's apprentice. He is the only person in the city who would still open a door to you.
But a door is exactly the problem. You found it out under the Institute's culvert: you cannot cross into a home unless someone who lives there asks you in.
The Watch is out in force. Bodies have been going missing from Weirside, and everyone is looking for a body-snatcher. Reach his window. Knock. Be asked in.

on enter z_dock: hint "Carry a body [C] to the water's edge and drop it. The Weir takes it, and a missing man raises no alarm."

on spotted any: hint "Seen! Break their line of sight, get into the dark. They will search, then give up."
on kill any: hint "Bodies get found. Hide them, or carry them [C] to the canal."

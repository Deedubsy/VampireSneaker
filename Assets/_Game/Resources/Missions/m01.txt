@script
on start: say Ilse "Cold... so cold." ; say Ilse "The needle. Saule's needle. I felt my heart stop." ; say Ilse "The others under those sheets... Ward C. My patients. None of them woke." ; say Ilse "And now... I can hear the orderlies' hearts." ; hint "[W][A][S][D] move Ilse. Walking is heard by anyone close: hold {Sneak} to sneak, slow and silent. Hold {Run} to run: fast, but loud. The arrow keys or a middle-drag look ahead." Sneak
on start: bark o1 "Last of Ward C. Doctor wants 'em burned before matins." ; timer t_doors 12
on timer t_doors: hint "Doors are shut. Walk up to one and press {Interact} to open or close it. Sneaking, you ease it open without a creak. Orderlies open doors as they come and shut them behind them." Interact ; timer t_intro 14
on timer t_intro: hint "Darkness hides you. Inside a pool of light, they can see you from much farther away." ; timer t_blood 14
on timer t_blood: hint "Your blood is almost gone. Blood fuels everything you are. Feed, or you will not last long."
on enter z_corr: hint "Hold {ShowCones} to see every vision cone. The bright inner band sees you even in the dark. The outer band only sees you in light." ShowCones
on enter z_ward: say Ilse "He's asleep. I can hear his heart from here." ; hint "Feed from behind, or on the sleeping. {Sip} Sip: take a little, leave them dazed but alive. {Drain} Drain: take everything. They die." Sip
on feed o_doze: say Ilse "Warm... I could have taken all of it." ; hint "Sipping leaves a witness who will wake, confused. Draining leaves a body. Bodies get found."
on kill o_doze: say Ilse "I didn't mean— no. I did."
on enter z_bay: hint "Wait for the lantern to pass. Patrols repeat. Learn the rhythm, then move. The rings on the floor show how far your footsteps carry." 
on enter z_store: hint "Crates, wardrobes and coffins hide you, and bodies too. Walk up to one and press {Interact}: Hide inside. Carrying a body: Hide the body." Interact
on enter z_boiler: reveal wheel ; say Ilse "The stoker. And on the box behind him... a valve wheel." ; hint "Sneak up behind him while he tends the fire: he hears footsteps, not a sneak. Drainpipes lead up: walk into one to climb it, or press {Traverse} beside it." Sneak ; bark o_stoker "Cold as a grave down here. Fire won't take."
on enter z_ante: reveal wheel ; say Ilse "The culvert gate. The valve beside it lifts it, if it still has its wheel." ; hint "Noisy actions draw anyone inside the ring. Wait until they are far away, or not able to come."
on interact wheel: unseal v1 ; say Ilse "Heavy. It will scream when I turn it. Then I must be quick." ; bark o_stoker "Where's my— who's been at the box?"
on interact v1: say Ilse "Now. Go." ; bark o3 "The gate? Who's down there?" ; group gate_alarm ; bark o_rush1 "That's the culvert! Down, quick!"
on interact n_record: say Ilse "'Deceased.' They wrote it down before I'd finished dying." ; say Ilse "'Heart restarted. Not reported.' Someone knew. Who is 'C'?" ; lore patient41 "Patient 41" "Your own file. 'Heart restarted 3:15. Not reported. — C.' Someone at the Institute saw you come back and kept it quiet."
on secret s_effects: say Ilse "Tobias. He doesn't know. He can't know what I am."
on interact n_orders: say Ilse "Saule. 'Any subject showing colour.' He expected one of us to wake."
on spotted any: hint "Seen! Break their line of sight and get into the dark. They will search, then give up."
on kill any: hint "A body. If another human sees it, the whole ward is roused. Carry it {Carry} somewhere dark, or hide it in a crate or wardrobe with {Interact}."
on complete escape: say Ilse "The canal. The city. Tobias..."

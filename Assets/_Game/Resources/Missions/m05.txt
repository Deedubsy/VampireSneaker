#! the boat at the Coal Wharf.
#! Teaches gas mains (a valve darkens its whole lamp group), lamplighters who walk to a cut main and reopen it, and
#! carrying a live victim (a Sip leaves her dazed for ~45 s; she does not wake while carried).
#! Layout: the Gasworks Cut along the south (start SW on the steps, the boat on the coal jetty SE of centre).
#!   West — the gasworks yard: two gasholders (a pipe climbs No. 1: secret shrine on top), the retort house (an
#!   always-lit furnace mouth), coal heaps, and the governor house with the WORKS main (yard + Gasworks Row lamps).
#!   Centre — the Guildhall: clerks' office and Penrose's study to the north, the great hall (gasoliers) to the
#!   south, Guild Square in front (a Vigil envoy at the door). The GUILD main is in the north alley behind it, and
#!   feeds the hall and the square. East — the lamplighters' depot: the apprentices' bunkhouse (Tobias's locker),
#!   the oil store, and the depot yard with the WHARF main (quay lamps). Guild Street and three terraces separate
#!   the square from the wharf.
#! Three ways to Penrose: the wall tops (the guildhall's walls run to the study), the back door from the north alley,
#!   or shut the guild main: she storms out to the alley to see to it herself.

Ostmere. The first night of the curfew.
The Pale Vigil came in on the morning tide: hunters in grey, with silver at their belts, under an Inquisitor-Captain named Vane. The Council of Lanterns brought them, and the Council of Lanterns has no faces.
Aurelia Penrose has one. She is Guildmaster of the Lamplighters: her guild decides which streets of Ostmere are lit, and which are left dark "by order". Someone gives her those orders.
Take her alive from her guildhall and carry her to the boat at the Coal Wharf. She cannot answer questions if she is dead.
Tobias is on shift tonight, somewhere on the wharf circuit. Keep him out of this.

@script
on start: say Ilse "The Lamplighters' Guildhall. Every lit street in Ostmere is lit from here, and every dark one is dark from here." ; say Ilse "Penrose knows who gives the orders. She'll tell me, if she's alive to do it." ; timer t_intro 5
on timer t_intro: hint "Gas mains feed whole streets. Shut a main {Interact} and every lamp on it dies at once. But lamplighters know their mains: one who finds a dark street walks to its valve and opens it again."
on enter z_works: say Ilse "The gasworks. The whole district breathes through these pipes." ; hint "The WORKS main is at the governor house. It feeds this yard and Gasworks Row. The retort furnace burns all night, whatever you do to the gas."
on interact gm1: say Ilse "Gasworks Row goes dark." ; bark fen "The works main! Who's been at the governor?"
on interact gm2: say Ilse "The gasoliers gutter out. The whole hall is dark." ; bark penrose "The hall's gone dark! Moss! Has nobody the wit to see to the main?" ; route penrose r_pen_main ; timer t_pen 30
on timer t_pen: bark penrose "Must I do everything in this guild myself?" ; route penrose r_pen
on interact gm3: say Ilse "The wharf goes dark. My way to the boat." ; hint "The WHARF main is Tobias's circuit. When he finds his lamps out, he'll come to the depot to open it again."
on complete mains: say Ilse "Three mains. Gasworks, guildhall, wharf. The whole Cut is blind tonight."
on enter z_hall: say Ilse "The great hall. They test the burners here: every new lamp in the city is lit first under this roof."
on enter z_study: say Ilse "Her study. The Council's money smells of lamp oil."
on interact n_letter: say Ilse "'Light his way.' They're feeding the Vigil to me, and they don't even know it."
on interact n_circuits: say Ilse "T. Marrow, apprentice. The Coal Wharf. That's where the boat is." ; hint "Tobias walks the wharf lamps. If he sees you, or you hurt him, you lose him. Wait for him to pass, or lure him off."
on feed penrose: say Ilse "Not too much. She has to be able to talk." ; hint "Penrose is dazed. Carry her [C] to the boat at the coal jetty before she comes round. She won't wake while you're carrying her; set her down, and the clock runs."
on kill penrose: say Ilse "No. No, no. She was the only one who knew."
on spotted envoy: say Ilse "Grey coat. Silver at his belt. The Vigil." ; hint "The Pale Vigil: hunters look up at rooftops and throw flares into suspicious darkness. Their bolts are silver."
on enter z_wharf: say Ilse "The coal jetty. Gethin's skiff is waiting where they said it would be."
on spotted tobias: fail tobias ; bark tobias "Ilse? ...Ilse! Ilse, your eyes, what have they — HELP! SOMEBODY!" ; say Ilse "Toby, no. Not like this."
on feed tobias: fail tobias ; say Ilse "Toby. Toby, I'm sorry. I'm so sorry."
on fail tobias: hint "Tobias saw what she has become. He'll remember this."
on enter z_bunk: say Ilse "The apprentices' bunkhouse. His bed's empty. He's on the wharf circuit."
on secret s_locker: vitae 5 ; lore tobias_locker "Tobias's locker" "A letter Tobias never sent: 'Come back.' The guild means to make him a journeyman by spring." ; campaign_flag tobias_letter
on secret s_holder: vitae 10 ; lore abbess_gasholder "The second shrine" "Thirteen marks scratched on a gasholder's crown around an open mouth: 'She talks to you in the heat. She says she is cold down there.'" ; campaign_flag found_shrine2
on interact n_works: say Ilse "'Dark streets at the Institute's back door.' Weirside went dark the week I died."
on lockdown: say Ilse "Whistles. The whole Cut is turning out." ; hint "Lockdown: every guard in the district is searching. Get out of sight and wait for it to pass."
on complete kidnap: say Ilse "Row, Gethin. Don't look at her, and don't look at me." ; say Penrose "You... you're the Weirside thing. Please. I only light the lamps." ; say Ilse "Then you'll tell me who tells you where."

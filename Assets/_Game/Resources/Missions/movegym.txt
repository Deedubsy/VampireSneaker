#! MOVEGYM — the corner-and-door gym for the WASD Movement Test (GAMEPLAY_REDESIGN §44). Not a campaign night: a
#! course of the things that catch a walking character. Load it with Game.Root.StartMission("movegym") (dev), with
#! Settings → Gameplay → Movement test mode on to log it.
#! South-west: a jagged diagonal corridor (stepped walls, the classic snag).
#! North-west: a one-tile corridor with four right-angle turns, and a door straight after the last turn.
#! North: three rooms in a row, their doors offset so each crossing turns, one door right in a corner.
#! Middle: a yard of crates and pillars to weave through, walked by one guard.
#! East: a hall with an S-bend, a post at its north end and a guard pacing the bend; the exit at its north-east.

# VESPERTINE — Campaign

14 missions, 4 acts. Each act escalates: **enemy knowledge, light infrastructure, and Ilse's power**.

Legend: **[Teaches]** new mechanics · **[Unlocks]** progression · **[Counter]** new enemy countermeasure ·
**[World]** narrative or world-state change.

---

## Story premise
Ostmere, the winter of 1871. Nurse **Ilse Marrow** discovers that the Coldwater Institute's "fever ward" patients
are being bled and injected with something black. Dr. **Emeric Saule** makes her a test subject and she dies. Then
she wakes in the flooded lower ward, starving, among the corpses of failed subjects. Above her, the Institute is
being "cleansed" after a failed experiment.

In blood-dreams, an ancient voice calls itself **the Abbess**. It is the vampire chained in the Institute's vault,
whose blood Saule uses. She tells Ilse: *"You are my daughter now. Come and find me."*

Ilse wants three things, which pull against each other:
1. **Truth & revenge**: who ordered this (the **Council of Lanterns**) and how to stop it.
2. **Tobias**: her 17-year-old brother, a lamplighter's apprentice. He is exactly the kind of person the city will
   send to hunt her.
3. **Hunger**: the Abbess offers power, and the price is becoming what the city fears.

The antagonists escalate with her:
- **Sergeant Pell** (City Watch). Act I. A tired man who thinks it's a wild animal.
- **Guildmaster Aurelia Penrose** (Lamplighters). Her guild "protects the light" and is secretly
  paid by the Council. Tobias's employer.
- **Inquisitor-Captain Aldric Vane** (The Pale Vigil). A former soldier of the old Order, summoned in Act II.
  Methodical, studies Ilse, writes the **Dossier**.
- **Sister Maud Hollin**. The Vigil's tracker. A predator who hunts with hounds. Ilse's mirror.
- **Dr. Emeric Saule**. The architect. Believes he is curing death.
- **The Abbess**. The final question.

## ACT I — PREY ("I need to avoid them")
Weak Ilse: 40 HP, 60 blood max, no abilities at first. Watch and orderlies with lanterns. One musket shot nearly
kills her. Darkness is the only friend she has.

### M01 — The Drowned Ward *(Escape)*
Coldwater Institute, flooded basement ward → maintenance culvert.
- **Objective:** Escape through the culvert gate into the canal tunnels.
- **Optional:** Find your own patient record (story secret, +1 Mark). Feed without killing anyone (*Mercy*).
- **[Teaches]** Movement, sneaking (footsteps are heard), doors, darkness = safety, cones (near/far bands),
  Sip-feeding from behind, hiding herself and bodies.
- **Flow:** Wake on a slab, its sheet thrown back, among the shrouded Ward C dead laid out for the furnace.
  Orderlies are clearing the bodies. The corridor door to the culvert antechamber is locked, and the gate valve's
  wheel has been taken off: the stoker keeps it on his tool box behind him in the fire-lit boiler room. Reach the
  boiler room through the linen store, the records corridor (lit, holds the secret) or the flooded ward (dark,
  long, past a dozing orderly who is the first feed). Sneak up while he tends the fire (or feed on him), take the
  wheel, then fit it at the gate. It screams: two orderlies run down from the records office, and the bars take a
  few seconds to rise (D123).
- **Special:** Starving start: blood 10/60. The first feed is staged (dozing orderly) and teaches Sip vs Drain.

### M02 — Lantern Street *(Infiltration / Reach)*
The slum of Weirside at night. Gas lamps, alleys, washing lines, the Watch.
- **Objective:** Reach Tobias's lodging-house. Ilse can't enter uninvited, so she must **get Tobias's attention**
  by knocking at his window (a climb-point route) while the watchmen are away.
- **Optional:** Drain Sergeant Pell's informer (notable). Retrieve the Institute coach's ledger from the stable.
  Never be spotted.
- **[Teaches]** Lamps (snuff), the far band (lit vs dark), bodies and evidence, carrying and dumping into the canal,
  the threshold rule, climb points.
  **First ability: Beckon** (D127). The Abbess first reaches her in a waking dream after her first feed of the night
  (or after 150 s if she hasn't fed), and grants Beckon there. A hint teaches it.
- **[World]** Tobias invites her in. Story beat: he's terrified but helps her. He mentions Penrose's guild is
  "working with the Institute".

### M03 — The Fishmarket Hunger *(Theft)*
Ostmere docks, fish market, harbour office. Drunk sailors, sick beggars, Watch, a dockmaster's office.
- **Objective:** Steal the Institute shipping manifest from the Harbourmaster's office (2nd floor, lit).
- **Optional:** Feed on 3 different blood types (*Connoisseur*). Dump 2 bodies in the harbour. Find the Abbess's
  first shrine (secret).
- **[Teaches]** Blood qualities (drunk, fevered), running water, and Beckon across the water. In the Abbess's second
  contact she says men can be sent across the river. The mission still grants Beckon, for a save that skipped M02.
- **[Unlocks]** Awakening 2. The Blood Arts have been open since M01 (D127).

### M04 — The Bells of Saint Corvin *(Sabotage)*
The Cathedral Quarter. Priests, acolytes, Watch, three bell towers that form the district's alarm network.
- **Objective:** Silence the three alarm bells (cut the ropes) *before* infiltrating the deacon's archive, then
  steal the baptismal registry of fever-ward patients.
- **Optional:** Never enter holy light. Spare every priest.
- **[Teaches]** Holy auras (no abilities inside), bells as alarms (an alerted guard runs to a bell), verticality:
  towers, drainpipes, rooftops. Light *types*.
- **[Counter]** Priests with censers of holy flame.
- **[World]** End of Act I: the city calls it "the Weirside Beast". The Watch doubles patrols.

## ACT II — HUNTED ("I can kill one if I'm clever" → "I can manipulate them")
The Pale Vigil arrives. Curfews begin. Ilse's toolkit opens up (2–3 active slots).

### M05 — The Guildhall of Lamps *(Kidnap)*
The Lamplighters' Guildhall and gasworks yard. Gas mains feed whole streets.
- **Objective:** Kidnap Guildmaster Penrose alive (knock out with Sip or Mesmerise, carry to the boat) to learn
  the Council's names.
- **Optional:** Shut the three gas mains (district blackout). Keep Tobias out of it (he's on shift: if he sees
  you, he panics. Don't let him be hurt).
- **[Teaches]** Lamp **groups** & gas valves (whole-section blackouts), lamplighters relight, carrying a live
  victim.
- **[Counter]** Lamplighters on patrol relight.

### M06 — Masquerade at Ashcombe House *(Intelligence / Social infiltration)*
A Council member's estate during a winter masque. Guests, servants, mirrors, private rooms (thresholds).
- **Objective:** Eavesdrop on three conversations to identify the Council members (stand in the shadows near
  the speakers for the full exchange). Then reach the study.
- **Optional:** Make Lord Ashcombe's death look like an accident (the balcony rail). Feed only on drunk guests.
- **[Teaches]** **Dominion**: thralls as the key into private rooms (they invite her). Crowds of civilians,
  panic, social distraction.
- **[World]** Names: Lord Ashcombe, Magistrate Crane, Bishop Lowell, and "the Lantern-Master".

### M07 — The Toll Bridges *(Traverse / Escape)*
After Ashcombe, the Vigil identifies Ilse. A curfew is called and the bridges are checkpointed. Ilse must cross
the river district to reach the fens.
- **Objective:** Cross three fortified bridges (running water: no other way, unless rooftops connect) to reach
  the fen road.
- **Optional:** Free the Faithful held at the checkpoint (if Rumour ≥ 2). Steal a Vigil field manual (reveals
  the Dossier system to the player).
- **[Teaches]** **Vigil hunters** (Look Up, flares, silver crossbows), **hounds** (smell, blood trails),
  checkpoints, Lockdown reinforcements.
- **[Counter]** Dossier countermeasures switch on from here.
- **As built:** four banks (Tanners' Row, Chandlers' Island, Ostbank Wharf, the fens) across the Mill Leat, the
  Ost and the Fen Cut, each with 2–3 crossings of different cost. Jory's skiff is unsealed by dealing with Jory in
  any way (`down`). The fourth Abbess shrine lies in the mill's wheel pit. Warden Cobb's conversation names Sister
  Hollin and Marrowmere (sets `heard_hollin`).

### M08 — Hollin's Hunt *(Special encounter: predator vs predator)*
The drowned fen village of Marrowmere (Ilse's family's village), mist and reeds, a night of rain.
- **Objective:** Sister Maud Hollin and her hounds hunt Ilse. Kill or enthrall Hollin.
  Hollin is Wary from the start, Looks Up, carries flares and is immune to Beckon. Her hounds follow blood.
  Isolate her from her hounds (feed on them? no: lure them into the bog / into the burning reeds), then take her.
- **Optional:** Don't lose more than 50% HP. Recover the family heirloom (secret story).
- **[Teaches]** Being hunted; using the hunter's knowledge against her (she follows *blood trails*, so lay false
  trails with Sanguis or leave a dazed victim as bait).
- **[World]** If Hollin is enthralled she becomes a powerful permanent thrall-informant in Act III (special
  dialogue, gives Vigil patrol routes).
- **As built:** a 64×44 drowned fen in rain squalls. From the SW causeway (Old Tamsin's hut) east: the reed beds
  behind the peat drain (one plank bridge), the drowned village (St Aud's and its tower with the fifth shrine; the
  Marrow house with Ilse's mother's locket and Hollin's candle), the Marrow Cut (footbridge, weir wall, sluice
  bridge), then the Vigil camp (tithe barn with Hollin's journal, tents, kennels, watch platform) and the drove road
  exit. Hollin hunts with two hounds from 50 s in (`hunts=18`); reading the candle card gives her an instant fix.
  She can be fed on (tearing her ward) and enthralled, killed, or led over the sawn drain planks or the sluice
  bridge, both of which sweep her away (`kill … accident`). Optionals: keep above half health, the locket, the
  journal, spare the hounds, the fifth shrine. Flags: `hollin_thrall` / `hollin_dead`, `marrow_locket`,
  `hollin_journal`, `hollin_past`, `found_shrine5`.

## ACT III — PREDATOR ("I can hunt groups")
Ilse has 3–4 slots and Awakening 5–7. The Institute is ready for her: sunstone lamps, garlic smoke, wards.

### M09 — Coldwater, Above *(Rescue)*
The Institute's upper wards and laboratories.
- **Objective:** Free **Clement** (Saule's assistant, who saved Ilse's record) and 3 imprisoned experimental
  subjects (fledgling vampires who are weak and can't climb; escort them or turn them loose to cause chaos).
- **Optional:** Destroy Saule's research notes. Feed on an alchemist.
- **[Teaches]** **Escort/protect** and **sunstone lamps** (lethal light; can't be snuffed, only switched off at
  the generator or broken by a thrall).
- **[Counter]** Alchemists with garlic censers.
- **As built:** 64×44, the night before a dawn "cleansing". Ilse starts at the east end of Coldwater Lane.
  - **Way out:** Tobias waits with a Guild cart at the west end of the lane; the escort and escape rect is there.
  - **The grounds:**
    - the coal yard (west, under a sunstone, with an orderly's beat)
    - the main gate (two sunstones and two Vigil hunters)
    - the physic garden (a lantern hunter)
    - the generator house (east), with **Mr Ambrose** the engineer posted beside the breaker for every sunstone
  - **The upper floor:**
    - the long gallery (two sunstones)
    - **Ward C** (west): three cells off a corridor sealed by two radius-7 sunstones, the orderlies' station
      (eavesdrop: Clement's fate, "number nine") and the restraint room
    - Saule's **laboratory** (centre): a sunstone, an alchemist and a scholar (eavesdrop: the undercroft under
      the cathedral), with an observation gallery above (the **sixth shrine**) and the subject register
      ("IX — MARROW, I. — DECEASED. Record closed. C.")
    - the **east wing**: the library, Clement's quiet room under guard, the dispensary, and Saule's locked study
      (key at the orderlies' station) where the notes are burned (`use notes`)
  - **The design:**
    - Every fledgling route out of the ward and along the gallery crosses burning light. Throw the breaker
      (Ambrose restores it within seconds unless he's dealt with) or have a thrall smash lamps.
    - Clement is human and walks the sunstone freely, but he is a civilian at walking pace on a long route
      past the dispensary and through the garden or gallery.
    - Two subjects out (or turned loose) satisfies the primary; all three alive is an optional.
    - Turning one loose (Drain key) pays Terror and the Abbess approves.
  - Campaign flags: `clement_saved`, `saule_notes_burned`, `knows_undercroft`, `found_shrine6`.

### M10 — The Gasworks *(Sabotage — world-changing)*
The vast gasworks and the sunstone lamp manufactory by the river.
- **Objective:** Destroy the sunstone lamp crates (3) and blow the main gas holder → **citywide blackout** for
  M11–M14 (world state: street lamps are off; the city brings torches, braziers and searchlights instead).
- **Optional:** Get every worker out before the explosion (Rumour +). Or don't (Terror +).
- **[Teaches]** Large vertical industrial level, timed sequence after the fuse is lit (escape).
- **As built:** 64×48, from Gasworks Road (north wall, main and side gates) south to the river.
  - **Districts:**
    - west: the lamp manufactory (consignment one, the drying gallery, the alchemist's office with the sunstone formula)
    - north-centre: the loading yard (consignment two on a dray, searchlight tower one)
    - north-east: the mess and coal sheds
    - centre: the retort house (furnaces, the charging stage, the works whistle)
    - east: the gas holder (a 9 m brick tank in an iron frame, the valve house at its foot, tower two; the seventh
      shrine on its top)
    - south-west: the coke yard and weighbridge office
    - south-east: the engine house (the searchlight dynamo)
    - south: the wharf (consignment three, tower three, Tobias's boat at the west end)
  - **Sequence:** burning all three consignments unseals the fuse. The fuse starts a 60 s countdown. The blast kills
    everything within 24 m of the holder (Ilse included) and wrecks it. It also lights the holder fires, cuts the
    searchlights and the works lamps, then 2.5 s later every street lamp in the city
    (`campaign_flag blackout`). Escaping before the timer runs out sets the holder off at once.
  - **Workers:** 8 gasworkers with `evac=`, sent by scares or by the whistle (noise 24 m, which draws the guards).
    All 8 out is Rumour +1; failing the objective is Terror +1.
  - **Lore:** the formula (`sunstone_formula`), the Guild seal on the wharf manifest (`knows_guild_seal`) and the
    seventh shrine (`found_shrine7`).

### M11 — The Opera of Lanterns *(Coordinated assassination)*
The Grand Opera House during the gala. Three Council members in three boxes.
- **Objective:** Eliminate Magistrate Crane, Bishop Lowell and Lord Halloway **within 90 seconds of each other**
  (or the survivors flee to the Vigil). This is built around **thralls held in place**: set them beside their marks, then strike in quick succession.
- **Optional:** Make one death look like the Vigil did it (False Orders from a thrall to a hunter), so the Watch
  and Vigil begin to distrust each other. This disables Watch–Vigil shouting for the rest of the act.
- **[Teaches]** Thrall control under pressure, crowds, near-simultaneous kills.
- **As built:** 64×48, the first mission in the blackout. Braziers, torches and two searchlights replace the lamps.
  - **Districts:**
    - west: Stage Door Lane (the start; the stage door; the cloakroom's lane door; the escape at its north end)
    - north: the stage house (a lit stage behind a 12 m arch; wings with stairs up to the box tiers; the fly gallery
      along the back wall with the eighth shrine; the dressing room; the dock door)
    - centre: the house (20 stalls guests facing the stage in the dark; the orchestra pit; the west and east box
      tiers; the royal box; the grand tier behind)
    - south: the foyer (the sergeant, the chandelier), the cloakroom (a gala mask: the Watch and guests take her for a guest; the Vigil does not) and the bar
    - east: Halloway's private stair down to Lantern Street, the Vigil's coach yard (searchlight two, a hound, the
      coachmen) and the coach house
    - far south: the square (searchlight one, the front doors)
  - **The targets:** Crane sits in the west tier beside his Vigil bodyguard (the frame). Lowell sits in the royal box
    in a holy aura. Halloway walks between his seat and the rail. His balustrade can be loosened: the next time he
    leans on it he falls into the stalls (an accident, not a kill).
  - **The window:** the first death starts a 90 s countdown. When it runs out, or on a lockdown, the house lights
    come up and the survivors run for the coach yard. Halloway gets there in about 15 s, Lowell in about 25 s. Any of
    them reaching it loses the night.
  - **The swell:** every 80 s (the first after 45 s) the orchestra swells for 10 s and noise carries a quarter as
    far. That is the moment for a loud kill.
  - **Optionals:** framing the Vigil (`watch_vigil_split`; Rumour +1 at the end), harming no guests (fail: Terror
    +1), the box plan in the cloakroom, the eighth shrine in the flies. Halloway's note on Vane (`knows_vane_distrust`).

### M12 — Vane's Bastion *(Infiltrate an enemy who knows your tricks)*
The Vigil fortress on an island prison-monastery. Running water all around.
- **Objective:** Reach the bastion (drawbridge thrall, a boat, or the prison's sewer outfall). Steal Vane's
  complete Dossier. Then **assassinate Vane** or **leave him alive and burn the archive** (choice).
- **Optional:** Free the imprisoned Faithful. No alarm.
- **[Teaches]** Maximum Dossier counters (all of your habits are countered), holy light grids, wards.
  The level is designed so **every build has a route** but none is easy.
- **[World]** If Vane is alive he leads the final defence personally (M13–14 harder but alternative ending).
- **As built:** 64×52. The river Vesper surrounds St Calder's island; the mainland (the start, the Vigil bridgehead,
  the jetty, the boatman) runs along the south. A 3 m curtain wall with a wall-walk rings the island.
  - **Three crossings:**
    - the weir: a wall across the river on the west, up onto the ledge below the prison. Wall-crawl
      (Awakening 4) gets her onto the curtain, or she wrenches the outfall grate (loud) into the prison.
    - the skiff: down the boatman by any means, then ferry to the east water-stair. A second skiff ferries her
      back.
    - the causeway: the portcullis is a windlass gate. Brother Anselm is a lay brother with no ward: enthrall him
      and order him to the windlass.
  - **The island:**
    - the prison (west): three cells of the Faithful, a jailer with the cell key, the drowned cell with the ninth
      shrine, and the postern to the west ledge.
    - the barracks and the kennels.
    - the bailey: braziers and the west searchlight.
    - the keep (north): the great hall, the chapel with the sanctuary lamp, the generator, and the archive upstairs
      under three sunstones. The keep searchlight is on the grid.
    - the cloister (east).
  - **Vane** walks study → archive → chapel, where he kneels under the sanctuary lamp for 35 s. He is holy and
    warded. Loosening the lamp chain kills him as an accident.
  - **The Dossier:** taken while Vane lives, it opens a choice:
    - kill him (`vane.kill`).
    - leave him alive (`vane.burn`): firing the archive presses sets `archive_burned`, and smoke raises a lockdown
      40 s later.
    - If he's already dead, taking the Dossier settles it.
  - **Leaving:** reach the mainland east of the reeds. On the way out, `vane_dead` (+1 Terror) or `vane_alive` is
    recorded.
  - **Full Dossier:** the header `dossier = all` fields every countermeasure with weight ≥ 2. Dormant groups add
    three wall sentries, two patrol partners, an inquisitor and two censer-bearers.
  - **Optionals:**
    - free the Faithful through the postern (Rumour +1, `faithful_bastion`).
    - no alarm on the island.
    - the ninth shrine (`found_shrine9`).
    - Vane's nightbook (`read_vane_book`).

## ACT IV — APEX ("I am terrifying, but careless play can still get me killed")
Awakening 8–10, 5–6 slots. The city is in lockdown. Humans hide from Ilse.

### M13 — The Long Night *(Hunt groups / Traverse)*
Three districts under curfew and martial law: hunter squads in formation, barricades, searchlights, fearful
civilians locked behind doors. Ilse must reach the cathedral before dawn.
- **Objective:** Break the Vigil cordon at three points (eliminate or scatter three squads) to open the way to
  the cathedral.
- **Optional:** Use only Terror (no kills: scatter the squads with panic). Protect Tobias's barricade (if he
  lives).
- **[Teaches]** Group hunting: squads move in formation, cover each other's backs, and respond as a unit
  (if one is taken, the others form a ring).
- **As built:** 72×62, south to north: the Wick (the start, Quay Lane, Sallow and Low Streets, the shut-up Marrow
  house, Candle Row), the Mercy canal (three bridges), the market district, and the cathedral close.
  - **The cordon:** three hunter squads, each sharing one nerve.
    - the bridge squad (Vigil, 4–5) walks Quay Lane, Mercy Bridge and the north quay.
    - the market squad (Vigil, 5–6) loops the market square under the searchlight tower.
    - the close squad (Vigil, 4) walks before the west doors. If `vane_alive`, Vane leads it himself: +30 nerve,
      and when his men break he stays and hunts her alone.
    - The `break` objective needs all three broken: routed by fear or cut down to the last man. Breaking it unseals
      the west doors.
  - **The district:**
    - barricades half-close Lantern Street, Rope Walk and Weavers' Bridge.
    - the Watch-house on Tanner Row holds Nell Cray in its lock-up (key on the sergeant's table).
    - the Hallorans bar their door. A drunk and a beggar are still out after curfew.
    - wall stairs at both ends of Lantern Street lead onto the close wall.
  - **Candle Row:** unless Tobias is lost, he and the Faithful have barricaded Candle Row. The Lock Street militia
    squad (60 nerve) crosses Lock Bridge to storm it on a 180 s countdown. Breaking them in time keeps him
    (`candle_row_held`, Rumour +1). Otherwise Tobias is lost (`tobias lost`).
  - **Optionals:**
    - Terror only: break them without a death.
    - hold the barricade (if Tobias lives).
    - free Nell Cray and bring her home to the Wick (Rumour +1).
  - **Flags read:** `vane_alive`, Tobias's state (`tobias_alive`, `tobias_thrall`, `tobias_lost`), countermeasures
    (`cm_rooftop` wall sentries, `cm_paired` an extra man in two squads, `cm_inquest`, `cm_censer`).
  - **Flags written:** `vane_dead` (killing him there; Terror +1), `candle_row_held`, `heard_singing` (the
    singing under the chancel, at the close listen point).

### M14 — The Abbess Beneath *(Finale, 3 stages)*
1. **The Cathedral at Dawn.** Sunbeams creep across the nave as time passes. Vane (if alive) holds the nave.
2. **The Undercroft.** Saule's laboratory, the source of the eternal-vitae trade. Saule is protected by a
   sunstone ring and wards; turn his own security against him.
3. **The Vault.** The Abbess, chained. Choice:
   - **Free her** → *Endless Night* ending (Ostmere falls to the dark; Ilse is the Abbess's heir).
   - **Consume her** → *The New Abbess* ending (Ilse becomes the apex; the city's fear becomes a cult).
   - **Destroy her, and the vitae** → *Dawn* ending. If Tobias lives and Rumour > Terror, Ilse walks into the sun
     beside him, or vanishes into the fens (variant).

**As built (2026-10-02), 64×64:**
- **Stage 1, the nave.** Three ways in: the locked west door, the bricked stair behind the chancel (an 8 s,
  noise-16 deed), and the sexton's stair under the south aisle (its key is in the vestry; Ashby opens it if the
  Faithful hold the bastion, `cf_faithful_bastion`). Four sunbeams start between 5:00 and 10:00 and creep across
  the floor, and the nave squad sweeps under them. Vane (`cf_vane_alive`) leads that squad.
- **Stage 2, the undercroft.** It is reached through the ossuary (the tenth shrine is there) or the charnel. Saule
  walks a loop from his desk to the sunstone ring to the purge chamber, with a warder at his heel.
  - The `gen_lab` generator in the boiler room cuts the ring and the lab lamps.
  - The `purge` lime-feed trap, armed once he steps into the chamber, kills him by "accident" (optional `purge`,
    sets `saule_purged`).
  - Thralling him sets `saule_spared`, and so does dropping him without killing him.
- **Stage 3, the vault.** The winch at Saule's desk opens the water-gate. The vault holds the vitae vats, the
  Council's men (a squad and Pike's gallery), three fledgling prisoners (optional escort to the river door), and
  the Abbess under a grate moonbeam. She is sealed until Saule is dealt with. Then comes the choice:
  - **free** and **consume** win after a short exchange.
  - **destroy** lights the burn group, reveals the hidden `flee` objective and starts a 50 s countdown to reach
    the river door. A blast on timeout loses the night.
- Every branch was verified in play to its ending screen, including the epilogue.

**Epilogue (`CampaignState.Epilogue`):** after the ending's own lines, one line each for Tobias, Vane, Hollin,
Saule, Clement, the Council, the Faithful and the shrines (n of 10), chosen from the campaign flags. Each line
appears only when its flag says something happened.

# VESPERTINE — Decision Log

| # | Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | 2026-10-01 | Title **Vespertine**, protagonist **Ilse Marrow**, city **Ostmere 1871** | Gaslamp era makes *light infrastructure* a central system (lamps, gas mains, lamplighters, later sunstone lamps). A newly turned nurse gives a believable weak start and personal stakes (Tobias). |
| D2 | 2026-10-01 | **Single protagonist + thralls**, no squad | Coordination is something the vampire *earns* (Dominion), which makes the progression arc itself the "squad". |
| D3 | 2026-10-01 | **Two-band vision cones** (near: sees in any light; far: sees only if lit) | Ties stealth directly to light manipulation and reads clearly. Original twist on the cone convention. |
| D4 | 2026-10-01 | **Dossier countermeasures** driven by player habits | "The enemy learns" becomes personal and counters dominant strategies diegetically. |
| D5 | 2026-10-01 | No morality meter; **Terror vs Rumour** world consequences | Both have mechanical upsides and downsides, so neither is "good". |
| D6 | 2026-10-01 | **Runtime-built text maps**, one scene | Fast authoring through the CLI, deterministic saves, consistent art, data-driven. |
| D7 | 2026-10-01 | **Two NavMesh agent types** (Humanoid, Vampire) | Lets thresholds, mist passages and vampire-only climb links exist without hacks; humans genuinely can't follow. |
| D8 | 2026-10-01 | **Loadout slots** (2→6) | Prevents late-game god mode; makes builds and replays meaningful. |
| D9 | 2026-10-01 | Health regenerates only in darkness *by spending blood* | Healing becomes a blood decision, and darkness matters even after detection. |
| D10 | 2026-10-01 | Classic vampire laws as mechanics: running water, thresholds, sun/holy, garlic, silver | Vampiric identity beyond the skin; generates level constraints and thrall uses. |
| D11 | 2026-10-01 | UI Toolkit built in code with USS | Text-authored, scalable, no prefab wrangling via CLI. |
| D12 | 2026-10-01 | Procedurally synthesised placeholder audio; primitive-built characters | No asset pipeline; still supports gameplay audio cues. Listed for replacement. |
| D13 | 2026-10-01 | Spot lights with shadows for static lamps (downward / outward from walls) | Rendered light pools must match gameplay LOS (no light bleeding through walls → readability). |
| D14 | 2026-10-01 | Runtime **NavMeshBuilder** (two agent types) instead of baked NavMesh assets | Maps are built from text at load; doors/invitations/thresholds rebuild the vampire mesh asynchronously. After every async rebuild the vampire off-mesh links are re-registered (UpdateNavMeshDataAsync disconnects them). |
| D15 | 2026-10-01 | Verb keys **F** Sip / **R** Drain / **C** Carry / **G** Use | Left hand on WASD-adjacent keys; Drain on a separate key prevents accidental kills. |
| D16 | 2026-10-01 | *(Superseded by D115.)* **Nightplan** intercepts ability/move input while active and queues it per unit | One input path for live play and planning; execution replays the same PlayerAction structs. |
| D17 | 2026-10-01 | Single scene with a code-built **GameRoot** (Level, AI, Mission, Audio, UI, Nightplan, Cones, Sense) | No scene YAML authoring through the CLI; clean teardown between missions. |
| D18 | 2026-10-01 | Mission header `blood` is the absolute starting blood | Designers control scarcity per mission directly. |
| D19 | 2026-10-01 | Render ambient and gameplay light are separate | Scenes may be readable on screen while gameplay darkness stays dark; LightSystem never samples RenderSettings. |
| D20 | 2026-10-01 | **MissionValidator** + a test per shipped mission file | Typos in ids/routes/actions fail CI instead of failing silently in play. |
| D21 | 2026-10-01 | `friendly` NPCs (Tobias) never perceive, hear or count as prey | Story allies can share space with the player without special-casing the AI. |
| D22 | 2026-10-01 | `zone … home=<door>`: a home blocks the vampire mesh until its door is invited | The threshold law expressed with level data, re-using the door-invite rebuild path. |
| D23 | 2026-10-01 | `hidden` objectives are primaries revealed by script; only `optional` is optional | Lets missions unfold in stages (knock → enter) without winning early. |
| D24 | 2026-10-01 | The world pauses while closing dialogue plays; the debrief waits for it | Story lines were being cut off by the debrief clearing subtitles. |
| D25 | 2026-10-01 | Blood Sense / Scent / Dread Feast share one **x-ray heartbeat renderer** | One readable language ("hearts through walls") for all sensing; pulse speed shows fear. |
| D26 | 2026-10-01 | Story gifts via the `grant` script action; the Blood Arts tab stays locked until M03 or the first gift | The Abbess teaches Beckon in a dream in M03, so the skill tree arrives as a story beat instead of a menu at minute one. |
| D27 | 2026-10-01 | Skiffs are `boat` interactables with `every interact … ferry x y` (fade + teleport) | Crossing running water is a vampire taboo; the boat is the diegetic exception, and needs no nav link over the canal. |
| D28 | 2026-10-01 | `count=` on multi-part objectives and a `dispose` objective type | "Feed on three of five kinds" and "give two bodies to the harbour" without bespoke script. |
| D29 | 2026-10-01 | `sealed` interactables, unsealed by script, instead of locked doors for "do X before Y" | Wall tops are continuous walkways, so a locked door never seals a room from a vampire. Sealing the objective item itself orders the mission without fighting the verticality. |
| D30 | 2026-10-01 | `route <npc> <route>` script action (saved with the NPC) | Story beats move people: the deacon leaves his archive when the bells fall silent, opening the room on a rhythm the player can read. |
| D31 | 2026-10-01 | `if` conditions on script rules (flags / completed objectives, `!` negation) | Context-sensitive hints and branches ("the register is under his hand — cut the bells first") without new events. |
| D32 | 2026-10-01 | Every `no*` objective plus `protect` is a conduct objective | nokill_type, nolockdown and protect previously never completed on a win, and a primary protect blocked the win. |
| D33 | 2026-10-01 | Lockdown reinforcements are generated from `spawn` points with deterministic ids, not authored as dormant groups | Every mission gets the GDD's reinforcement rule with two lines of authoring; deterministic ids (`rf_<point>_<n>`) let a save rebuild them and restore each by id, which fixes K1. |
| D34 | 2026-10-01 | A shut gas main marks its lamps `GasCut`: they cannot be relit at the lamp, so a lamplighter walks to the group's valve and reopens it | Mains are the "whole-section blackout" tool, and the lamplighter is their counter. Reopening at the valve keeps the counterplay legible (a man walking to a wheel) and gives the player a window to read and deny. |
| D35 | 2026-10-01 | Setting a body down inside an active `deliver` rect never tips it into nearby water | Boats and jetties sit beside water, and the canal-dump radius (2.6 m) covered the whole jetty, so every delivery drowned the victim. |
| D36 | 2026-10-01 | Vigil reinforcements arrive from M07 (or header `vigil = 1`), not from the start of Act II | The Vigil arrives in Ostmere in M05, but it doesn't hunt Ilse until the Dossier opens in M07. Before that, a lockdown is the Watch. |
| D37 | 2026-10-01 | A mask is an item with explicit rules (walk, floor, no feeding/carrying/mist, no restricted zones), and fooled NPCs see nothing at all | Social stealth has to be readable. A partial detection multiplier would leave the player guessing; one clear on/off state, with the HUD naming the broken rule, makes the masque a puzzle instead of a gamble. The Vigil and Warded NPCs are never fooled, so Vane stays a threat in the middle of a party. |
| D38 | 2026-10-01 | Story gifts (`grant`) are free, survive a respec and aren't auto-slotted when they're thrall commands | The Abbess hands over Enthrall in M06. Charging marks for a gift, or refunding it on respec, would contradict the fiction; auto-slotting a sub-command would push out an ability the player chose. |
| D39 | 2026-10-01 | Arranged accidents are `trap` interactables (rig it, then a listed victim walks into it); an `accident` death raises investigation, not alarm | Shadow Tactics' environmental kills, reframed as the vampire who can't be seen to kill. Whitelisting victims keeps traps from becoming general-purpose mines. The `kill … accident` objective rewards patience over a bite. |
| D40 | 2026-10-01 | A home zone lists every threshold door (`home=d1,d2`); one invitation opens them all | A house has more than one door. The thrall-at-the-kitchen-door route and the invitation-at-the-front route must lead into the same house. |
| D41 | 2026-10-01 | Thralls invite her in from the door cell or the cell beside it (2.4 m) | Players order a thrall to the doorstep, not into the doorway. At 1.8 m a thrall standing on the step did nothing. |
| D42 | 2026-10-01 | Only the 8 lit lamps nearest the camera cast real-time shadows (5 on Hard shadows), re-ranked every 0.3 s | M06 has 40+ lamps; URP shrank every shadow map to fit the atlas until none of them read. Shadows matter where the player is looking, and lamps far from the camera are small on screen. Fixes K3. |
| D43 | 2026-10-01 | Blood trails are discrete, sequenced drops a hound follows forward and spends | A continuous scent field is opaque. Drops are visible, can be outrun or stopped (mist, rest), and a hound that doubles back would read as broken. |
| D44 | 2026-10-01 | Countermeasures are concrete rule changes, each stated in the Dossier in the Vigil's own words (caged lamps, paired patrols, 25 s wake, wards, censers, salt, rooftop sentries) | The player must be able to read exactly what changed and why, so they can change habits on purpose. |
| D45 | 2026-10-01 | Script and objective conditions may use world flags (`rumour`, `terror`, `cm_*`, `cf_*`); objectives take `if=` | Campaign state (reputation, countermeasures, earlier choices) has to reshape a mission without one file per branch. |
| D46 | 2026-10-01 | Before the Vigil field manual is stolen (M07 optional) the Dossier shows only rumour words, not weights | Turns the Dossier itself into a reward, and gives the M07 optional a mechanical payoff. |
| D47 | 2026-10-01 | A `down <npc>` event fires the first time an NPC is dazed, enthralled or killed, by any means | Scripts like "the ferry is free once the ferryman is dealt with" must not care which verb the player chose. |
| D48 | 2026-10-01 | `follow=<npc>` heel behaviour for hounds (hysteresis 0.7/2 m, catches up at 1.25x, runs beyond 6 m) | Two NPCs on the same route drift apart in seconds. A hound and its handler must read as a unit and break up only when the player breaks them up. Also fixed: entity `Link()` (paired=/follow=) was never called. |
| D49 | 2026-10-01 | Nav build `minRegionArea` 0.4 m², and the async vampire rebuild uses the same settings as the first build | Lone 2 m wall tops eroded below the 2 m² default and vanished from nav, so the M07 toll arch was unclimbable. The async rebuild used default voxel and tile sizes. |
| D50 | 2026-10-01 | A hunting tracker takes periodic, tightening scent fixes rather than seeing or tracking Ilse continuously | A boss who always knows where she is turns the level into a chase. Fixes give the hunt a rhythm the player can read (bark, walk, cast about, repeat) and turn "where will she look next" into a plan. Hiding or mist resets it, so the player always has an out. |
| D51 | 2026-10-01 | Rain is a stealth state (half smell range, trails wash away in 30 s, quieter steps, looser fixes) that comes and goes in scripted squalls | Weather that changes the rules gives the player a reason to wait, and the squall rhythm makes waiting a choice rather than a chore. |
| D52 | 2026-10-01 | Feeding on a Warded NPC tears the ward; Dominion then works on them | A Warded boss who is immune to every Dominion route leaves only the kill. Tearing the ward on a feed keeps the enthral ending possible, but only for a player who got close enough to bite. |
| D53 | 2026-10-01 | `take <npc>` completes on a kill (any cause) or an enthral; `thrall <npc>` lets the script tell them apart | M08 must accept every way of dealing with Hollin, including a sprung trap, while the campaign remembers whether she lives on as a thrall. |
| D54 | 2026-10-01 | A tracker's hounds cast about at each fix, then return to heel; an enthralled handler's hounds calm at once | The cast-about window is the counterplay that separates Hollin from her dogs without a scripted lure. A thralled handler whose dogs still hunt Ilse would read as a bug. |
| D55 | 2026-10-01 | Traps can `sweep` the body away (running water); traps never take hounds | The sluice and the drain planks must not leave a body on the bridge. Hounds are too light to spring a sawn plank, and an "accident" that kills a dog is not one the Vigil would believe. |
| D56 | 2026-10-01 | `GameEvents.ClearMissionSubscribers` also clears `NpcDowned`, and the MissionController unsubscribes in `OnDestroy` | The Editor enters Play mode without a domain reload, so static events outlive the play session. A destroyed controller from an earlier session stayed subscribed and threw on the next `take`. |
| D57 | 2026-10-01 | A priest's holy aura (6 m) suppresses Ilse's holy-blocked abilities but does not burn; only holy flame and sunstone lights burn | Resolves K6. A burning aura on a walking NPC makes every priest a moving damage zone and leaves Predator players with no answer. Suppression keeps priests as a puzzle (Predator verbs, the pounce and rend, still work; Silverblood frees the Sanguis tree), while burning stays tied to lights the player can read and put out. |
| D58 | 2026-10-01 | Abilities are verified by a play-mode sweep (`DevAbilitySweep`) in parts, each on a fresh mission load | A single pass contaminates itself: early checks leave bodies, panicked witnesses and dark lamps that turn later checks into vacuous passes. Each check also asserts its own precondition (the witness really saw her, the lamp really was relit) so that a pass means something. |
| D59 | 2026-10-01 | Corpse puppets cannot relay False Orders without Living Lie | The node promised this, but nothing enforced it, so the first half of a tier IV passive did nothing. The dead silence of a puppet is also the right default: a corpse that gives orders should cost something. |
| D60 | 2026-10-01 | Dread presence (Awakening 9): a citizen who sees Ilse, or is terrified by her, panics without a scream or a bark | It was in the Awakening table but never implemented. Late in the campaign the city's fear of her becomes a stealth tool: citizens scatter silently instead of raising the street. Panics spread by hearing a scream still scream. |
| D61 | 2026-10-01 | Escort: a `prisoner` NPC waits in shackles (`<id>.free`), then follows her on foot. Prisoners cannot climb, are recaptured by any guard who reaches them, and leave the map at the escort rect (`<id>.out`) | Rescue is the Act III turn ("humans hide from me", but she still protects her own). Followers that can't climb force a ground route, and recapture on contact gives guards a counter that costs time, not the mission. Following prisoners string out in a line (2.2 m plus 1.1 m per place) so a group reads as a group. |
| D62 | 2026-10-01 | Fledglings (undead prisoners) balk at the edge of burning light and back out of it; they can be turned loose (Drain key) to hunt the staff. They die on a guard who is already Alerted or Searching, and take anyone else | The sunstone ward is the puzzle: cut the generator or smash a lamp first. Turning them loose is the predator's answer, and it pays Terror. A frenzy that dies on the first armed man is worthless; one that dies on a guard already hunting it makes alarms the counter. |
| D63 | 2026-10-01 | Sunstone lamps can't be snuffed, smothered or relit at the lamp. They are cut from a `generator` (which an engineer restores) or smashed for good by a thrall (`<id>.smash`, unless `nosmash`) | Burning light needs a counter that isn't free. The generator is a big, temporary, contested blackout; smashing is permanent and local, and it needs a thrall because the light burns her. |
| D64 | 2026-10-01 | Prisoners and loose fledglings ignore panic, alarms, distractions and searches (every state entry returns while `Rescue`) | Captive Clement and a chained fledgling panicked and ran across the map when the frenzy started. A prisoner's state belongs to the escort system alone. |
| D65 | 2026-10-01 | Generic `use` interactable (verb/time/noise, optional prop) for one-off scripted deeds; `verb=`/`name=` text replaces underscores | Burning Saule's notes needed no new class. Fixed along the way: M08 trap verbs showed underscores ("Draw_the_sluice"). |
| D66 | 2026-10-01 | Searchlights are a GameLight that moves (the pool), aimed by a manned lamp; the operator sees the pool at any range and his alert state steers it | Reusing the light field means every guard, the HUD light meter and the burn rules treat the pool as a lamp at no extra cost. | D67 | 2026-10-01 | Searchlight Unity intensity scales with the square of the throw | Inverse-square falloff made the pool from a 20 m tower almost invisible next to a gas lamp, while the gameplay light was full strength. The visual must match what the guards see. |
| D68 | 2026-10-01 | Evacuation is panic with a destination (`evac=`), and evacuated NPCs simply leave (inactive); no new save field | A worker who has fled is exactly a deactivated entity, which the save already restores. | D69 | 2026-10-01 | `blast` kills count as Ilse's kills (Terror, debrief) | She lit the fuse. Getting the workers out first is the way to keep a clean night, which is the moral choice M10 is built around. |
| D70 | 2026-10-01 | Static prop swaps (`swapprop`) persist as `#swap|x|y|type` mission flags instead of a new save section | Props are static meshes, not entities. A flag reuses the existing save list, and the load path re-applies swaps after the level rebuild. |
| D71 | 2026-10-01 | M10 start moved off the wharf lamp and out of tower three's sweep | The first build started Ilse in the escape rect (a "still work to do" toast at once), under a gas lamp, and in the wharf beam's path: an idle player died in 12 s. A mission start must be dark and quiet. |

| D76 | 2026-10-01 | A 12 m proscenium arch with one curtain prop, not a 24 m open stage | Two curtain props across a wide opening put their valances between the camera and the stage; the narrow arch frames the lit stage and leaves the wings as hidden space. |

| D79 | 2026-10-01 | `choice` answers are `<id>.<key>`, set as a mission flag and a campaign flag, and fire a `choice` event; the validator knows the offered keys | `id:key` broke rule parsing, which splits headers at the first `:`. | D80 | 2026-10-01 | Stairs prefer a raised neighbour with open floor behind them (a straight flight) before the highest neighbour | The keep stair in M12 ran between two 6 m walls and laddered onto a wall top, so Vane's study-to-chapel walk went round the curtain (158 m instead of 38 m). | D100 | 2026-10-02 | The earning curve is tuned by simulation over the shipped missions. Objective Vitae is paid on first completion. Notable Marks are paid once per notable.
